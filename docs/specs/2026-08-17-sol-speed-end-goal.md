# Sol speed — end goal

Speed is the spine. Gravity and air only matter if you can read how fast you are going, and if the engines are in the same units.

## What you should hold in your head

You spawn parked over Earth. The meter says **0 m/s**. You fall. The number creeps. That is gravity.

Burn sideways hard enough and the number settles near **3 km/s** — geostationary circle. Burn more and you climb. Cut engines and you coast. Vacuum does not slow you.

Dive into the air. Below **100 km** the meter stops climbing for free. Drag eats speed. Down low you fly like a plane, about **80 m/s** on the engines. Climb and the same engines go faster as the air thins. Above the skin you are in space again.

Hit the ground and you stop. You do not land. Later a planet generator will paint a close-to-real surface when you are near. Still no landing.

Other star systems stay arcade until we say otherwise.
